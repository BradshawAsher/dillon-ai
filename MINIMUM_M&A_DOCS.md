To ensure your agent is performing a rigorous "Forensic Audit" rather than just data extraction, you should define a "Diligence Manifest" in your workflow.
If these documents are missing, your agent should automatically flag the deal as status: 'Needs Review' or escalated: true with the reason: "Incomplete Data Packet."
Here are the bare minimum files you should require for a verdict:
The "Must-Have" Diligence Manifest
3-Year Comparative Income Statement (P&L):
Why: Without this, you cannot determine growth trends, margin compression, or EBITDA consistency.
Validation: Your agent must be able to sum the years/quarters to match the total.
Balance Sheet (Most Recent + Prior Year):
Why: To see working capital trends, debt levels, and ensure assets aren't being "hidden" off-books.
Customer/Revenue Concentration Schedule:
Why: A company might have great revenue, but if 80% comes from one customer, the valuation is fundamentally fragile.
Validation: Your agent should flag any single customer $>20\%$ of total revenue.
Fixed Asset Register:
Why: This proves the company actually owns what they say they own. It highlights "hidden" capital expenditure needs (e.g., if everything is fully depreciated and about to break).
Accounts Receivable & Payable (AR/AP) Aging Report:
Why: Reveals "Hidden Debt." If AR is growing faster than revenue, the company isn't actually collecting its cash; it’s just booking "fake" sales.
